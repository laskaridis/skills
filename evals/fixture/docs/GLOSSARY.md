# Glossary (ubiquitous language)

- **Basket** — the collection of lines a customer intends to buy. We never say "cart".
- **Basket line** — one product with a quantity inside a Basket.
- **Guest** — a visitor without an account; identified by a session id.
- **Customer** — a visitor with an account.
- **Checkout** — converting a Basket into an Order (owned by another context, not yet built).
